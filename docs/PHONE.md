# Alphonso on your phone

The Alphonso app lets you check on your computer's work from your phone: chat with Alphonso,
see the requests on the board, approve or reject a step that waits for you, and stop, carry
on or cancel a request. Your computer still does all the work and makes every decision; the
phone only asks.

Teams, Skills, Settings and connectors stay on the computer for now; the More tab lists them
as "On your computer for now".

## What you need

- Alphonso on your computer, open, with the same account signed in as on the phone.
- An iPhone with iOS 16.4 or later, or an Android phone with Android 8.0 or later.
- An internet connection on both. They don't need to be on the same Wi-Fi.

## Install

During testing the app comes as a test build: TestFlight on an iPhone, and an email invite
from Firebase App Distribution on Android. Open the invite on the phone and follow it. It
will be in the App Store and Google Play later.

## Sign in

1. **On the computer:** Settings → Runtime → Remote Access → **Phones** → **Sign in to pair a
   phone**. Your browser opens the Alphonso sign-in page; sign in with Google or an email link.
2. **On the phone:** open the app and sign in with the same account: Apple, Google, or your
   email address (the app sends a link; open it on the phone).

## Try a demo

Not paired yet? Tap **Try a demo** on the sign-in screen. You see sample requests, a sample chat and a
sample Miya Studio project with its calendar, so you can look around. A bar at the top says
"Demo · sample data, nothing is sent": every action answers "This is a demo: nothing is sent.", nothing
reaches any computer, and nothing is kept on the phone. Tap **Leave demo** to go back to sign-in.

## Pair

1. On the computer, in Phones, choose **Pair a phone**. A QR code appears. It works once,
   for 10 minutes.
2. On the phone, tap **Scan the QR code** and point the camera at it.
   Pairing never asks for a 6-digit PIN. The PIN and QR code under **Older iPhone app (PIN
   pairing)** on the same page are for the older Alphonso Companion app; the phone app says
   "That QR code isn't from Alphonso" if you scan that one.
   If you can't scan, choose **Copy the code** on the computer, send the code to your phone
   in a way you trust, and paste it in the app's **Pair** box.
3. The phone appears in the computer's list with when it was last used.

A code from another app is refused, and the app says where to find the right one.

## What you can do from the phone

- **Chat:** ask Alphonso anything; bigger requests are planned by Jose on the computer, as in
  the desktop Chat.
- **Requests:** the board in one column. Approve or reject a step that waits for you; Stop a
  running request; Carry on one that was cut off; Cancel one; run or cancel a plan that waits.
  Tap a step to read its work in full: Hector's report with its numbered sources, a review's
  findings, the exact text Marcus will send and where. On a send you can **Send back** the
  writing with a reason (the agent redoes it and the send asks you again) or **Edit text**
  yourself. A change Alphonso wants to make to your code is approved on your computer only.
- **Studio:** Miya Studio's projects, the one waiting for you first. Approve a review or
  **Send back** with what to change, pick another version, watch the videos and see the
  pictures, edit a post's caption, hashtags and YouTube title, and **Approve and schedule**
  (Face ID or your fingerprint first). **New project** starts one from an idea, a brand and
  what to make.
- **Calendar:** your posts by day or by week, with brand and platform filters. Tap a post to
  see it, change its time, or cancel it (cancelling asks for Face ID or your fingerprint).
  Moving a post already scheduled on YouTube or Facebook asks first, as on the computer.
  Content Studio drafts and Marcus's messages are changed on the computer.
- **More → Memory:** what Echo remembers, newest first, with a search your computer answers.
  When Echo suggests tidying up, **Apply** or **Dismiss** it here. To add something, say
  "Remember that …" in Chat (the keyboard's microphone works); forgetting or editing a
  decision is done on the computer.
- **More → Projects:** your saved projects. **Start** a run, open its latest run on the
  Requests board (approve its waiting steps there), or make a **New project** with the same
  questions as the computer's Projects page.
- **More → Boardroom:** the threads, newest first. Open one to read its last 30 messages
  (**Show earlier** for more), write with @mentions (the agents reply on your computer, as on
  the desktop), start a new thread, and run **Conclude**, **Distribute** (review only, or a
  Slack post that waits for your approval) and **Creative brief**. A message that needs your
  confirmation stays hidden until you tap **Reveal** and confirm with Face ID or your
  fingerprint. Boardroom only notifies you when a thread needs your decision; you can
  **Acknowledge** it here.
- **More → Teams:** your saved teams with their steps. Type what the team should do and
  tap **Run**; the run shows on the Requests board. Building or changing a team is done on
  the computer (System → Teams).
- **More → Skills:** each agent's skills, what they're for and how they've done, and any skill
  file your computer didn't use, with why. Writing skills is done on the computer.
- **More → Settings:** the same switches as Settings → Approvals on the computer (Approval
  Mode, what asks first, Allow paid models and its monthly limit, how long waiting requests
  last), Zero-Cost Mode, which model each agent uses, and which notifications this phone gets.
  Anything that makes Alphonso ask less or spend more (turning Approval Mode, an "ask before"
  switch or Zero-Cost Mode off, allowing paid models, raising the limit, giving an agent a paid
  model) asks for Face ID or your fingerprint first. Spending, sending and deleting always ask.
  Keys, passwords and prices are only set on the computer.
- **More → Connectors:** each connector's status and last check, with its on/off switch. One
  that needs a key says **Set up on your computer**.
- **Notifications:** when a step or plan waits for you, a request finishes or fails, or a
  review needs you. The text is opened only on your phone; Apple, Google and the relay see
  only "New activity".
  - **Approve** and **Reject** work right from the notification.
  - A step that **spends money, sends or publishes, or deletes** shows only **Open**: you
    approve it inside the app, after Face ID or your fingerprint.
  - **Ready to check:** when Miya finishes new pictures or a new video, you're told once,
    with a small picture of it when it fits in the notification. Tap it to open the project.

## Pictures and videos on the phone

Your computer makes a phone-sized copy (about 480p for a video) and sends it in pieces; the
phone checks each piece and the whole file before showing it. A video loads when you tap it.
On mobile data, one larger than 20 MB asks first and says its size. Copies are kept on the
phone until the post goes out.

Each phone can pass about 200 MB a day through Alphonso's relay. Previews stop a little
before that, so messages and approvals still get through; the app says when the limit resets.
Links in a report open in your phone's browser; one that looks disguised asks first.

## Face ID and fingerprint

The app asks for Face ID or your fingerprint when it opens. You can switch that off in
More. Approving a step that spends, sends or deletes always asks, whatever that setting.

## When your computer is off

The app says "Your computer is offline since 14:02." and shows what it last knew. Buttons
that need the computer are off until it is back. If something changed on the computer while
you were editing it on the phone, the phone says "This changed on your computer; here's the
latest." and shows it, so you never overwrite newer work. Nothing is lost: requests wait on the
computer as usual.

## Privacy

Messages between your phone and your computer are encrypted on one device and opened only
on the other. Alphonso's relay passes them on without being able to read them, and keeps
no content. Your computer refuses any device it hasn't paired, and only devices signed in
to your account can reach it.

## Remove a phone or switch phone access off

On the computer, Settings → Runtime → Remote Access → Phones:

- **Remove** a phone: it can't reach the computer again.
- Untick **Phone access** to stop all phones until you tick it again.

With no phone paired and no pairing code showing, the computer doesn't connect to the relay
at all.

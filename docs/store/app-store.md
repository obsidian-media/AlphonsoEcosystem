## Description

Alphonso is a team of AI agents that works on your own computer: Hector researches, Miya makes videos and pictures, Marcus prepares posts and messages, Maria and Sentinel check the work, and Jose plans who does what. This app brings that team to your phone.

The phone app works with the free Alphonso desktop app. Your computer does the work; the phone shows it and lets you decide.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Approve or reject a step before it sends, posts or spends anything, and see exactly what will go where.
• Chat with Alphonso and follow requests as they run.
• Open Miya Studio projects, look at pictures and videos, and approve reviews.
• See your posting calendar.
• Look at memory, projects, the Boardroom, teams, skills, settings and connectors.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Messages between your phone and your computer are end-to-end encrypted. Our relay passes them on without being able to read them.
• Face ID or your fingerprint protects the app and every approval that sends or spends.
• No ads, no tracking.

TRY IT FIRST
Not set up yet? Tap "Try a demo" on the sign-in screen to look around with sample data.

You need a computer running the Alphonso desktop app to use your own data.

## Review notes

The app connects to the user's own computer running the Alphonso desktop app, so a reviewer can't pair without one. To see the app working, tap "Try a demo" on the sign-in screen: it opens the real screens with built-in sample data (marked "Demo · sample data, nothing is sent" at the top), and every action answers "This is a demo: nothing is sent." Tap "Leave demo" to return.

Account deletion: sign in, then Delete my account at the bottom of the Pair screen (or More → Delete my account once paired). It explains what is deleted, asks for Face ID, and deletes the account through our relay.

Sign-in options: Sign in with Apple, Google, or an email link. No paid content or in-app purchases.

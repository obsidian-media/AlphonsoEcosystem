Alphonso privacy policy

Obsidian Media · Last updated 2 November 2026

Alphonso is a desktop app that runs on your own computer, and a phone app that connects to it. This policy says what each part handles. In short: your work stays on your computer, the phone and the computer talk with end-to-end encryption, and we keep only what is needed to connect them.

On your computer

The desktop app keeps your chats, requests, projects and settings on your computer. Keys you enter for outside services are kept in your computer's own keychain. When you connect an outside service (for example a search service, a social network or a paid AI model) and Alphonso uses it, what it sends goes to that service under its own terms. Apart from connecting to your phone (below), nothing from the desktop app is sent to us.

The phone app

  • Your sign-in. You sign in with Apple, Google or an email link, through Google's Firebase Authentication. It keeps your account id and your email address so the same account can sign in on your phone and your computer.
  • What connects your devices. Our relay (run on Google Cloud) passes messages between your phone and your computer. It stores only: which devices belong to your account, whether each is a phone or a computer, when each was last connected, which phone is paired with which computer, and your phone's notification token. It keeps no messages, requests, files, names of your projects or keys, and keeps no access log.
  • Your content is end-to-end encrypted. Everything between your phone and your computer, including notifications, is sealed on one device and opened only on the other. The relay passes it on without being able to read it.
  • Notifications go through Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple's push service, sealed. If the phone can't open one in time, it shows only "New activity".
  • Face ID and fingerprint are checked by your phone. The app only learns whether the check passed.
  • Pictures and videos you open on the phone are kept on the phone as previews.
  • The demo ("Try a demo") uses sample data built into the app. It sends nothing and keeps nothing.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell or share your data.
  • There is no advertising and no tracking, in either app.
  • We don't read your content: we can't, it is encrypted end to end.

Keeping and deleting

The relay's records are kept while your account exists. To delete your account, open the phone app and choose More → Delete my account. That deletes your sign-in and the relay's records for every phone and computer on your account. Your work on your computer is not touched; remove it there if you want it gone. To remove one phone only, use Settings → Runtime → Remote Access → Phones on your computer.

Children

Alphonso is not made for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date. Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub Issues (issues are public, so don't include private details).

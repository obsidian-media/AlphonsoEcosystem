Ask a question or report a problem

Open an issue on GitHub Issues. Say what you did, what you expected and what happened, and which phone and computer you use. Don't post keys, passwords or private messages: issues are public.

Getting started

  • The phone app works with the Alphonso desktop app on your computer. Install it, then on the computer open Settings → Runtime → Remote Access → Phones and sign in.
  • On the phone, sign in with the same account and scan the pairing code your computer shows.
  • Not ready to pair? Tap Try a demo on the phone's sign-in screen to look around with sample data. Nothing is sent from the demo.

Common questions

  • "Your computer is offline." The phone shows what it last knew. Start Alphonso on the computer and check that it is online; the phone reconnects by itself.
  • Remove a phone. On the computer: Settings → Runtime → Remote Access → Phones → Remove.
  • Delete your account. On the phone: More → Delete my account. See the privacy policy for what is deleted.

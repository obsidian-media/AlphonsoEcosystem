# Product Hunt Launch

## Title
Alphonso — Local-First AI Desktop Companion

## Tagline
9 agents, 25 connectors, iOS companion — AI that runs entirely on your machine.

## First Comment
We built Alphonso because we were tired of cloud-dependent AI assistants that send your data to someone else's server.

**What is Alphonso?**
A local-first AI desktop companion powered by Ollama. It runs entirely on your Windows machine — no cloud dependency for core operation. Think of it as your personal AI operating system with 9 specialized agents working together.

**Key features:**
- 🤖 **9 Specialized Agents** — Jose (orchestrator), Hector (research), Miya (creative), Maria (governance), Marcus (distribution), Echo (memory), Sentinel (security), Nova (analysis), and Alphonso (operator)
- 🔌 **25 Connectors** — Telegram, WhatsApp, YouTube, GitHub, Slack, Notion, ClickUp, Claude API, ChatGPT, and more
- 📱 **iOS Companion** — Pair your iPhone for mobile access with voice support
- 🎙️ **Voice OS** — Local speech-to-text and text-to-speech with persona-aware conversations
- 🏛️ **Boardroom** — Multi-agent group chat where all 9 agents collaborate on complex tasks
- 🔒 **Local-First** — Your data stays on your machine. SQLite storage, no cloud required
- 🔄 **Auto-Update** — Seamless in-app updates with signed manifests

**Who is it for?**
- Developers who want AI assistance without leaving their workflow
- Creators who need multi-agent orchestration for content production
- Privacy-conscious users who want AI without cloud dependency
- Teams exploring local-first AI with approval-gated execution

**Pricing:** Free for local use. Pro at $12/mo adds premium connectors and cloud fallback. Enterprise at $49/mo adds team features and compliance.

We've been building this for over a year with 3,758+ tests, full TypeScript migration, and a security-first architecture. Today we're opening it up to the world.

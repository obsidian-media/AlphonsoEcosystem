# Product Hunt Launch

## Title
Alphonso — Local-First AI Desktop Companion

## Tagline
9 agents, 33 connectors, idea-to-posted-video — AI that runs on your machine.

## First Comment
We built Alphonso because we were tired of cloud-dependent AI assistants that send your data to someone else's server.

**What is Alphonso?**
A local-first AI desktop companion powered by Ollama. It runs on your own computer (Windows, Mac or Linux) — no cloud dependency for core operation. Think of it as your personal AI operating system with 9 specialized agents working together.

**Key features:**
- 🤖 **9 Specialized Agents** — Jose (orchestrator), Hector (research), Miya (creative), Maria (governance), Marcus (distribution), Echo (memory), Sentinel (security), Nova (analysis), and Alphonso (operator)
- 🧭 **Jose plans every request** — as steps for the right agents, with approval by what each step does, a live plan board, and Telegram approvals
- 🎬 **Miya Studio** — from an idea to finished, posted videos, Shorts and carousels, with a Calendar and results
- 🔌 **33 Connectors** — Telegram, WhatsApp, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, GitHub, Slack, Notion, ClickUp, Claude, ChatGPT, and more
- 📱 **iOS Companion** — Pair your iPhone for mobile access with voice support
- 🎙️ **Voice OS** — Local speech-to-text and text-to-speech with persona-aware conversations
- 🏛️ **Boardroom** — Multi-agent group chat where all 9 agents collaborate on complex tasks
- 🔒 **Local-First** — Your data stays on your machine. SQLite storage, no cloud required
- 🔄 **Auto-Update** — Seamless in-app updates with signed manifests

**Who is it for?**
- Developers who want AI assistance without leaving their workflow
- Creators who need multi-agent orchestration for content production
- Privacy-conscious users who want AI without cloud dependency
- Teams exploring local-first AI with approval-gated execution

**Pricing:** Free at launch — every agent and connector, no license key. Paid services (Claude, ChatGPT, ElevenLabs, Higgsfield, Runway) are optional, use your own account, and always ask first with the cost.

We've been building this for over a year with 6,700+ tests, a desktop end-to-end suite against the real app, and a security-first architecture. Today we're opening it up to the world.

Download for Windows, Mac or Linux from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/obsidian-media/AlphonsoEcosystem/releases/latest) — the iOS companion is on TestFlight.

Happy to answer any questions! 🚀

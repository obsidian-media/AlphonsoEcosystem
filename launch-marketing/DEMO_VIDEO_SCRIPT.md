# 60-Second Demo Video Script: Alphonso v2.6.5

## "Your AI. Your Machine. Your Rules."

---

### Scene 1: The Problem (0:00 - 0:10)
**Visual:** Screen recording of a generic cloud AI chat interface. Text appears: "Your data. Their server."

**Voiceover:**
"Most AI assistants send your conversations to the cloud. Your data. Their server. What if your AI lived on your machine instead?"

---

### Scene 2: The Reveal (0:10 - 0:20)
**Visual:** Alphonso desktop app opens. Clean, dark UI with sidebar showing 9 agents. The Alphonso logo animates in.

**Voiceover:**
"Meet Alphonso. A local-first AI desktop companion that runs entirely on your Windows machine. No cloud required."

---

### Scene 3: Multi-Agent Magic (0:20 - 0:35)
**Visual:** User types in Boardroom: "Research the latest AI trends, create a summary, and post to Telegram." The screen shows Jose orchestrating — Hector researches, Miya formats, Marcus distributes. Each agent's avatar lights up as they work.

**Voiceover:**
"Nine specialized agents work together. Jose orchestrates. Hector researches. Miya creates. Marcus distributes. All coordinated through one conversation."

---

### Scene 4: Connectors & Voice (0:35 - 0:45)
**Visual:** Quick montage — Telegram bot responding, WhatsApp command /status, Voice OS waveform animation, iOS companion pairing screen.

**Voiceover:**
"Connect to Telegram, WhatsApp, YouTube, GitHub, Slack — 25 connectors. Talk to it with Voice OS. Pair your iPhone for mobile access."

---

### Scene 5: Privacy & Control (0:45 - 0:55)
**Visual:** Settings panel showing "Local-Only Mode" toggle. SQLite database icon. Approval workflow modal for a high-risk action.

**Voiceover:**
"Your data stays on your machine. Approval gates for risky actions. Full control. Full privacy."

---

### Scene 6: Call to Action (0:55 - 1:00)
**Visual:** Download button. GitHub stars counter. URL: github.com/obsidian-media/AlphonsoEcosystem

**Voiceover:**
"Alphonso. Free for local use. Download today. Link in description."

**Text on screen:**
```
Alphonso v2.6.5
github.com/obsidian-media/AlphonsoEcosystem
Free • Local-First • Open Source
```

---

## Production Notes
- **Resolution:** 1920x1080 (16:9)
- **Music:** Subtle, tech-forward ambient track (low volume under voiceover)
- **Voice:** Professional, warm, confident male or female voice
- **Captions:** Burned-in subtitles for accessibility
- **Total runtime:** 60 seconds exactly
- **Format:** MP4 (H.264) for YouTube, MOV for Product Hunt

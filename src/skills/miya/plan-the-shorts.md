Plan the Shorts for this video (the number asked for in Outputs, else one). Each Short is 30 to 60 seconds and has a hook of at most 12 words. Choose for each its kind: "mini" (its own short script: write its parts, each with the words spoken and a picture prompt), "highlight" (the strongest moment of the finished video, with a new hook spoken over its start: give the moment's start and end seconds within the finished video) or "cut" (a part of the finished video as it is: give its start and end). Prefer mini unless the finished video has a moment that works on its own. For each Short write its own copy per platform.

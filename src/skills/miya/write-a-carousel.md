Write a carousel of 5 to 10 slides from the research and the script: slide 1 is the cover with the hook, then one idea per slide, and the last slide is the call to action. Each slide has a headline of at most 8 words, a body of at most 25 words, and an image prompt for its picture (no text in the picture).
